Join us for a cèilidh in the Main Hall at The Club on **Saturday 14 November, from 7pm till late**.

Tickets are **AED 200 for Scottish Society members** and **AED 250 for non-members**, and include finger foods on the night. There's a cash bar for all standard drinks — [view the menu](https://qrco.de/ceilidhmenu).

Dress code is smart casual, and kilts are very much encouraged. The event is exclusively for guests aged 21 and over.

To book, email the committee using the button above and we'll confirm your tickets by reply. Please note there will be photo and video coverage on the night for the Society's marketing.
